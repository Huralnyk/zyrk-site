What to watch tonight?

The ball from Interstate 60, in your pocket. Shake the phone and it hands you one film out of the 1,000 best-rated on TMDB. One. No endless scrolling.

Coming soon to the App Store

  • Shake and watch

    The phone sloshes like a real liquid-filled ball, the triangle floats up — and there is a title on it. Not tonight? Shake again.

  • Never twice

    A film the ball has already shown will not come up again. Your Good and Nope marks tell it which way to lean next time.

  • Where to watch it

    Every film page lists the services that carry it in your country — subscription, rent or buy. Data from TMDB, the way JustWatch does it.

  • Nothing extra

    Free, no ads, no subscriptions, no sign-up. Your history stays on the device.
